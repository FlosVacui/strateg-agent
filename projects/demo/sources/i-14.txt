Prevent churn before subscribers leave
Stop losing telecom subscribers to competitors. Pushwoosh detects churn signals in real time and triggers personalized mobile push notifications before subscribers start looking elsewhere.
Behavioral churn scoring : Identify at-risk subscribers based on declining usage, missed payments, or reduced app engagement
Automated retention flows : Trigger loyalty offers, plan discounts, or personalized outreach the moment churn risk spikes
Contract renewal reminders : Proactively engage subscribers whose contracts are expiring with tailored upgrade or renewal incentives
So you can reduce voluntary churn by reaching subscribers with the right offer at the right time, turning potential cancellations into renewed commitments.
Segment subscribers by usage patterns and lifecycle
Go beyond basic subscriber demographics. Pushwoosh enables dynamic segmentation based on real usage behavior, plan type, and engagement patterns that matter to telecom marketers.
Data usage segments : Automatically categorize subscribers by data consumption patterns to identify upgrade candidates, heavy users approaching cap limits, and low-engagement users at churn risk
Plan and lifecycle targeting : Target prepaid vs. postpaid, new activations vs. long-term subscribers, and contract-ending users with messaging that matches their lifecycle stage
Event-based triggers : Respond instantly to subscriber events like roaming activation, data cap reached, or service disruption with contextual push notifications
Which means you can deliver hyper-relevant offers and alerts that resonate with each subscriber segment, increasing conversion rates and reducing irrelevant messaging.
Drive plan upgrades with personalized recommendations
Turn usage data into revenue. Pushwoosh analyzes subscriber behavior and sends timely push notifications with plan upgrade recommendations when subscribers are most likely to convert.
Usage-based upsell triggers : Automatically notify subscribers when their usage patterns suggest they would benefit from a higher-tier plan or add-on services
5G migration campaigns : Target eligible subscribers with personalized 5G upgrade offers highlighting coverage availability in their area and relevant plan benefits
Add-on service promotions : Promote international roaming packages, family plan additions, or premium content bundles based on individual subscriber profiles
This means you can increase ARPU by delivering upgrade suggestions that feel helpful rather than pushy, converting browsing subscribers into higher-value customers.
Automate payment reminders and reduce disconnections
Keep revenue flowing and subscribers connected. Build automated payment reminder sequences that reduce late payments and involuntary churn across your entire subscriber base.
Multi-step payment flows : Set up escalating reminder sequences (gentle reminder, due date alert, final notice) that adjust tone and urgency automatically
Prepaid balance alerts : Notify prepaid subscribers when their balance is running low, with one-tap top-up options that reduce service interruptions
Post-payment confirmations : Send instant payment confirmation notifications to build trust and reduce support calls about billing status
So you can accelerate collections, reduce involuntary disconnections, and lower call center volume by keeping subscribers informed at every step of the billing cycle.

FAQ
How do push notifications help reduce subscriber churn in telecom?
Push notifications enable proactive churn prevention by detecting behavioral signals (declining usage, missed payments, reduced app engagement) and automatically triggering personalized retention campaigns. Instead of waiting until a subscriber requests cancellation, Pushwoosh lets you intervene early with targeted offers, loyalty rewards, or plan adjustments.
Because push notifications appear directly on subscribers' devices even when your app is closed, they have significantly higher visibility than email or in-app messages alone. Telecom operators using Pushwoosh for proactive retention see up to 25% reduction in voluntary churn rates.
The key is combining behavioral segmentation with automated trigger campaigns: identifying the right subscribers at the right moment and delivering an offer that addresses their specific situation, whether it's a contract renewal discount, a plan downgrade option, or a loyalty bonus.
