Verizon Family Privacy Policy for Kids
What is Verizon Family?
If you use a Gizmo, please visit our Gizmo Privacy Policy for Kids.
Verizon Family lets parents (known in the app as Guardians) and other trusted adults (known as Members) keep an eye on kids' phones, tablets, and smartwatches. It helps families find each other's devices and even their pets. It also includes tools that let your Guardians (and others they allow) set screen time and usage limits; approve contacts; set to-dos for you and rewards; block certain websites and apps; pause the internet; see who and when you call and text over the Verizon network and what websites you visit and apps you use; monitor your driving activity; and connect your school calendar to their Verizon Family app. Verizon Family also has Safe Walk, which lets you quickly send your device location to your emergency contacts. Your Guardian can also turn on Roadside Assistance so that you can get help if you have a problem with a car you are in, like a flat tire or a dead battery, or Family Line, which is a phone number that can be shared by up to five family members.
What information do we collect about you?
When a Guardian adds you to a Verizon Family account, we collect from them your name, photo, birthday, trusted and emergency contacts, and info about the Verizon Family features they want to use, like whether they want to block certain websites or apps, and if you are a driver or passenger (if you have turned on Driving Insights monitoring). If you are using a tablet connected to the Verizon network, we may also collect your email address to send you directions about how to download the Verizon Family app.
The other types of information we collect depend on your Guardian’s plan, which features they turn on, your devices, and what you and your Guardian give us permission to do.
If your phone or tablet is connected to the Verizon network, we collect your device location using our wireless network, information about who and when you are calling and texting over the Verizon wireless network, how much data you are using, and information about your device, like its phone number and the type of device you are using.
If you download the Verizon Family companion app and give us permission, we collect your device’s precise location using GPS. If you are using a phone or tablet, we also collect the websites you visit and apps you use, the contacts saved on your phone or tablet, and your motion activity that shows if you are walking, running, biking, or riding in a car. We collect other information about your device, like the device type, the version of the Verizon Family app you are using, the features you use, other device identifiers and your IP address. The information we collect is used to provide the services requested by your Guardian.
If you enable Driving Insights, we will collect information about where you are and other information like your trip route and distance; how fast the vehicle traveled; how the vehicle braked, sped up, and cornered; and potential crashes. This information is used to create a driving score for each trip you take and to send speed and crash alerts to your Guardians or emergency contacts. If your Guardian says you are a driver, we will also collect info about whether you are using your phone when the car is moving and use that to help calculate your overall driving score.
If your Guardian has turned on Roadside Assistance, and you request help, we will collect your name, phone number, info about your car, the address where you are asking for help, and the service you need so that our Roadside Assistance provider can help you. Your Guardian can also assign you “to dos” and rewards for completing them and can connect your school calendar to Verizon Family.
If your Guardian invites you to Family Line, Verizon Family assigns a new phone line (the “Family Line”), which you will share with up to five family members. Verizon Family will collect and maintain your contacts to allow you to more easily make and receive phone calls, information about the calls you make and receive using the Family Line, voicemails left on the Family Line, and an address used for E-911 emergency services.
If your device is connected to the Verizon network, we will also collect an E-911 address to help with emergency response in case you need help.
How does Verizon Family use this information?
Verizon Family only collects from you the information we need to make Verizon Family work and to improve it. We never sell kids’ information or use it to show them ads.
Who does Verizon Family share this information with?
Verizon Family shares information about you with other people on the Verizon Family account and with companies that help us provide Verizon Family. These companies can only use your information to provide and help us improve Verizon Family and they must delete your information when they no longer need it to provide the service. We don't share information we collect through Verizon Family for any other purpose.
We share information about you, including where you are, with a company called Mapbox so they can show where you are on a map in the Verizon Family apps. We also share certain identifiers from your device with Google Analytics so it can help us see how our apps are working and with PubNub to deliver alerts that Guardians set about your location or activity. Information about your online activity on your phone or tablet may also be shared with a company called Cloudflare if a VPN is turned on to monitor the websites you visit and the apps you use or block you from visiting certain websites or apps. And if Driving Insights is turned on, we share your location, motion data, and information about phone usage while driving with a company called Cambridge Mobile Telematics who provides driving scores and speed alerts. If your Guardian enables Roadside Assistance and you request help, we will share your name, phone number, info about your car, the address where you are asking for help, and the service you need with our Roadside Assistance provider, Signature Motor Club.
What control do I have over the information you have about me?
When your Guardian adds you to their Verizon Family account, they have to agree to let us collect some information about you. They can also invite you to download the Verizon Family Companion app. If you download the app, you’ll be asked to let Verizon Family collect some information about you, like the precise location of your device, how you’re moving (like walking, biking, running, or in a vehicle), and the contacts on your phone or tablet. You’ll also be asked to let your Guardians and others they allow see what websites and apps you’re using, block certain ones, and pause the internet. The Companion app on your phone or tablet uses a Virtual Private Network or “VPN” to turn on these parental controls.
Depending on your Guardian’s Verizon Family plan, you may be asked to consent to the use of Parental Controls Protection to prevent you from deleting the Companion app or the VPN.
If you have an iOS device you will be asked to install a mobile device management (MDM) profile, which will also include the installation of the VPN. This MDM profile will stop you from deleting the VPN or the Companion app on your phone or tablet. To uninstall the Companion app or the VPN, the MDM profile must first be deleted from your device. If you have an Android device, you will be asked to install a VPN and to turn on Accessibility settings for the Companion app on your phone or tablet. If you turn on Accessibility settings, you won’t be able to delete the VPN or the Companion app without first asking your Guardian for a PIN that is found in their Verizon Family app. If you uninstall the VPN, some features that let your Guardians and others they allow see the websites you visit and apps you use may be turned off, but if you use an Android device your screen time info might still be collected unless you turn off your usage access permission in your device settings.
You can turn off some permissions, like the sharing of your motion activity or your contacts, in your phone, tablet, or watch's settings. You can also change your location permission in your phone, tablet, or watch’s settings. If you change your location permission, Verizon Family will still get your estimated location from the wireless network for your phone or tablet if you have a Verizon device. You can turn off Driving Insights monitoring in your Verizon Family app settings and remove yourself from Family Line.
If you delete the Verizon Family Companion app, Verizon Family will stop collecting GPS location, how you’re moving, online activity, and driving info. But Verizon Family will still get the estimated location from the Verizon wireless network of your smartphone or tablet.
To stop all information sharing by Verizon Family, your Guardian needs to remove your profile from their Verizon Family app. Verizon will continue to collect information to provide wireless service to your device.
How long does Verizon Family keep the information it collects about me?
We only keep the information for as long as we need it to provide the Verizon Family service to you. When your Guardians don’t want to use Verizon Family any longer and unsubscribe from Verizon Family, or delete your profile from their account, Verizon Family deletes the information we have about you unless we need it for legal or security purposes.
Do I have specific rights under any state privacy laws?
Some states give you the right to see what data we have about you and ask to have it deleted or corrected. If you would like to see the data we have about you or ask to have it deleted or corrected, talk to your parents. They can see, delete, and make changes to some information we have about you directly in their Verizon Family app or can make a request on your behalf. If you are 13 years or older, you can make a request through our Verizon privacy inquiry form.
